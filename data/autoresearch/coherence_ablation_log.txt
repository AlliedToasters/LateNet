## Best result: v2 at 40.7% (-12.6pp)

v2 was the cleanest improvement: coherence scoring on sibling+cousin swaps
with the full distant_swap pipeline unmodified. The distant_swap scoring in
later versions introduced noise because the distant pool is huge and the
scorer can't meaningfully differentiate across root-tree boundaries.

## What worked
1. **NDIF logit-weighted sampling** — directly addresses semantic incoherence
   by weighting candidates the model considers plausible as "X is a ___"
2. **Template selection** — hyp_02 ("is a type of") at 29% vs hyp_01 ("is a") at 50%
3. **Generic synset blocklist** — prevents "bridge is a type" etc.
4. **Sensitive lemma filter** — prevents culturally uncomfortable pairings

## What didn't work
1. **Source coherence pre-filter** (iter 1) — top_n=20 filtered nothing (0 skips)
   and cost 1100+ NDIF calls. Too expensive, too permissive.
2. **Top-fraction hard cutoff** (iter 3) — reduced diversity and concentrated
   sampling on a smaller set that included awkward candidates too
3. **Distant_swap scoring** — the distant pool is from different root trees,
   so the logit signal is noisy. The category-level filter already handles
   the structural quality; adding logit scoring didn't help much.

## Remaining awkwardness: word-sense layer (not coherence)
The ~40% remaining awkward FALSE statements break down as:
- **Obscure source senses**: "consequence" (phenomenon), "side" (region),
  "surface" (artifact), "spirit" (supernatural being) — WordNet senses
  that don't match colloquial meaning
- **Abstract category siblings**: "person is a cell" (biological cell is a
  sibling of person under organism), "location is matter" (sibling under object)
- **reverse_relation leaking sensitive content**: "A person is a type of Jew"

## Recommended next steps
1. **Abstract category depth filter**: source synsets at depth ≤ 3 in WordNet
   (person, location, body, water) produce the worst statements because they're
   too broad — their siblings span unrelated domains. Skip source synsets
   where min_depth ≤ 3 for sibling/cousin swaps.
2. **Synset concreteness score**: use the logit distribution entropy as a
   proxy — if the model's completions for "A {word} is a ___" are very spread
   out (high entropy), the word is too abstract for "X is a Y" framing.
3. **Wire reverse_relation through sensitive filter**: check both source AND
   target lemmas against the sensitive blocklist.
